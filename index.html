What Is Narinig Mo Ba?

Narinig Mo Ba? is a short, story-driven Filipino psychological horror game set inside a neighborhood sari-sari store.

You take the role of the store owner and spend your shift serving customers from the neighborhood.

At first, everything feels ordinary. Customers ask for products, you search the shelves, check prices, accept payments, and return the correct change.

But the conversations happening across the counter are just as important as the transactions.

Everyday gossip gradually becomes more meaningful. Names, relationships, debts, rumors, and seemingly harmless comments begin to connect, revealing a darker mystery hidden behind the familiar community.

Narinig Mo Ba? was originally created for Game Jam Los Baños 2026 and later received a post-game-jam version with improvements, browser support, and native desktop builds.

How to Play Narinig Mo Ba?

The simple sari-sari store routine gives the horror its shape. Read carefully and let the mystery develop naturally.

1. Listen to Customers

Read the complete request before selecting a product. The dialogue is an important part of the story.

2. Find the Product

Search the shelves, hanging sachets, refrigerator, and counter areas for the requested item.

3. Check the Price

Confirm the price when needed before returning the product to the counter.

4. Count the Change

Accept the customer's payment and select the correct Philippine peso bills and coins.

5. Follow the Conversation

Pay attention to names, rumors, debts, and recurring topics. Small details can become important later.

6. Avoid Spoilers

Narinig Mo Ba? works best when the mystery develops naturally during your first playthrough.

A Cozy Sari-Sari Store With a Darker Story

A sari-sari store is a familiar neighborhood shop in the Philippines—a place for everyday products, quick errands, and casual conversation.
